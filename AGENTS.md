# Image Generation Guidelines

When generating images (such as blog headers, diagrams, or assets):
- Use Orbitali's refined, futuristic visual identity: premium, minimal, spacious, and inspired by orbits, celestial motion, signal waves, and intelligent voice technology.
- Use the core brand palette: Ink (`#171419`) and Orbitali Purple (`#2D064F`) as the primary background and accent tones, supported by Porcelain (`#FAF8F6`), White (`#FFFFFF`), Mist Lilac (`#EEE8F1`), Orbital Lilac (`#B8A5C2`), Dust Purple (`#75647F`), and Warm Sand (`#F2EBDD`).
- Match Orbitali's product-site visual system by default: dark near-black backgrounds, fine low-contrast technical grids, subtle radial purple glows, crisp geometric structure, and high-contrast porcelain or white focal elements. Light compositions may use Porcelain backgrounds with Ink typography-like shapes and restrained purple accents when appropriate.
- Build each image around a clean, concrete visual metaphor for the blog post's actual technical flow or business concept. Favor waveform curves, orbital paths, connected nodes, architectural boundaries, routing diagrams, and purposeful spatial composition over screenshots, device mockups, or generic decoration.
- Prefer flat or lightly dimensional editorial illustration with matte surfaces, precise lines, controlled contrast, soft depth, and restrained luminosity. The result should feel like a premium developer platform rather than cinematic science-fiction concept art.
- Use stars, nebulae, planets, literal outer-space scenery, glass objects, and dramatic atmospheric effects only when the article specifically calls for them; never use them as the default expression of the Orbitali brand.
- Keep visual effects sparse. Avoid glossy 3D spectacle, excessive bloom, excessive glass, neon cyberpunk styling, clutter, cartoonish sci-fi imagery, and generic AI-core imagery.
- Preserve generous negative space and a sophisticated editorial composition suitable for a premium AI voice-agent company.
- Never include text on the image.
- Do not include the Orbitali wordmark or logo unless the user explicitly requests branded logo placement.
- Do not create central symbols that could be mistaken for the Orbitali logo or another AI company's logo.
- Use the content of the session/blog post to design the image so that it is a concrete visual metaphor of the technical concept.
