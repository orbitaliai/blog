# Image Generation Guidelines

When generating images (such as blog headers, diagrams, or assets):
- Always use a light, minimalist, Apple-like 3D rendering style.
- Use a color palette composed of an off-white/cream background with soft lavender and dusty purple accents.
- Focus on clean geometric lines, glass textures, and abstract metaphors representing software concepts rather than screenshots or device mockups.
- Never include text on the image.
- Use the content of the session to design the image so that it makes sense.

**Style Prompt Reference:**
- **Aesthetic**: Clean, spacious, high-end, and minimalist.
- **Background**: Solid off-white, light grey, or warm cream.
- **Palette**: Soft lavender, dusty purple, and lilac accents.
- **Composition**: High-precision 3D geometric primitives (smooth spheres, delicate glass elements/domes, and soft shadows). Abstract networks represented by thin, elegant wireframe lines, tracks, and light trails.
- **Constraints**: No text, labels, or device frames (laptops, phones, tablets) unless explicitly requested. High resolution with soft, diffuse lighting.
